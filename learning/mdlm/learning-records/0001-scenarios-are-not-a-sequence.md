# Scenarios are contracts, not a sequence

The learner correctly explained that successful publication must be followed by reevaluation and that Scenarios declare conditions rather than a traditional ordered workflow. They can use the broad operator loop, but they do not yet feel fluent with the terms or the deterministic path from an unsatisfied Obligation to the selected Assignment.

## Evidence

They scored 5/5 and explained the model in their own words. Their phrases "pick the scenario that makes most sense" and "may or may not have actually fulfilled everything required" show where the next lesson should sharpen the model.
