# Escalate an exhausted Question Decision correction

Read the exact Decision lineage, Question, and all supplied failed Reviews and
Findings. Tell the stakeholder that two replacement-and-Review cycles failed.
After renewed exact judgment, publish one same-lineage DEC preserving Decision
kind and exact Question scope, linking every failed Review through
`corrects-review`. Fresh independent Review remains required; escalation does not
make an unsupported answer or disposition applicable by itself.
