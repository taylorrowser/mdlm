# Correct reviewed candidate authority

Read `skills/lifecycle-data.md@1`, `skills/traceability.md@1`,
`skills/scope-challenge.md@2`, and `skills/author-preflight.md@2`.

Renew stakeholder judgment for the exact failed candidate-correction scope
Decision. Read the complete Decision lineage, every supplied failed Review, and
the exact corrected candidate. Publish one same-lineage DEC replacement that
keeps `effective_scope` fixed to that candidate and corrects every supplied
failed Review.

Do not revise the candidate, widen its scope, infer authority, hide a failed
finding, or repeat the rejected Decision unchanged. The replacement may clarify
or correct its rationale, alternatives, and decision text only within the exact
candidate scope. It receives fresh independent Review before gate signoff may
rely on it.
