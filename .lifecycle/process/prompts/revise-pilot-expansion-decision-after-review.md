# Revise a failed pilot Expansion Decision

Read the complete exact Decision lineage, its PAS and passing PAS Review, and every
failed Decision Review and Finding. After fresh explicit stakeholder authority,
publish one replacement DEC Revision in the same Stable Datum lineage. Preserve
the exact PAS recommendation and expansion scope, cite that PAS through
`justifies`, cite its passing Review through `relies-on-review`, and cite every
supplied Decision Review through `corrects-review`. The replacement requires a
fresh independent Review before proceed, change, or stop can apply. After two
causal replacements fail, this same attended interface is the explicit
exhausted-lineage escalation; do not invent another recovery route or any Phase
3–6 output.
