# Revise a failed pilot Expansion Decision

Read the exact Decision, its PAS and passing PAS Review, and every current failed
Decision Review and Finding. After fresh explicit stakeholder authority, publish
one replacement DEC Revision in the same Stable Datum lineage. Preserve the exact
PAS recommendation and expansion scope, cite that PAS through `justifies`, cite
its passing Review through `relies-on-review`, and cite every supplied failed
Decision Review through `corrects-review`. The replacement requires a fresh
independent Review before proceed, change, or stop can apply. Do not invent a
recovery route or any Phase 3–6 output.
