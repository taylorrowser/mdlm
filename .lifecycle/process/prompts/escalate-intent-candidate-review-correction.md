# Escalate an exhausted Phase 0 candidate correction

Read the exact candidate lineage, all supplied failed Reviews and findings,
`skills/lifecycle-data.md@1`, `skills/baseline-model.md@1`, and
`skills/scope-challenge.md@1`. After explicit stakeholder authority, publish one
same-lineage BSL replacement citing every failed Review and preserving every
unaffected member and evidence item. Publish one exact scope DEC justifying that
replacement. The replacement still requires a fresh independent Review and does
not itself approve, reject, defer, cancel, or terminate the gate.
