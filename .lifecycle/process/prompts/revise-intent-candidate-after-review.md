# Revise a failed intent candidate

Read `skills/lifecycle-data.md@1`, `skills/baseline-model.md@1`, the exact failed
candidate, its exact Review and Review Context, and the supplied reviewed
foundation members. Publish one frozen replacement Revision in the same BSL
lineage. Correct only the failed findings, preserve justified exact membership,
and link `supersedes` to the failed candidate Revision. The replacement requires
a fresh contextual Review before gate work can continue.
