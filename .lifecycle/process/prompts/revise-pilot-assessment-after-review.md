# Correct a failed pilot assessment

Revise the supplied PAS in the same stable lineage. Address only the exact failing
Review findings, retain the supplied frozen assessment context, and preserve the
failed Revision and Review as immutable history. Recompute no measurement without
exact evidence and do not promote qualification or pilot results into formal evidence.
Change the assessment rationale to explain the correction and link `corrects-review` to every
supplied failing Review; an unchanged payload or incomplete Review linkage cannot
satisfy correction. The corrected PAS requires a fresh independent Review before
any Expansion Decision.
