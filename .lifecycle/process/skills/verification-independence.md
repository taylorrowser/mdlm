# Verification independence

- Pilot and formal authoring are black-box relative to the controlled product boundary.
- Permitted inputs are exact requirements, strategy, relevant public boundaries, VER, controlled target, credentials, services, and data.
- Prohibit product source, product unit tests, private implementation details, and uncontrolled shortcuts.
- Record the exact authoring inputs actually used.
- Qualification may inspect ENV implementation only to establish declared environment capabilities.
