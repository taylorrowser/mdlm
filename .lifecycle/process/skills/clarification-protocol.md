# Clarification protocol

Classify uncertainty before acting:

- **Preferential:** only a stakeholder can choose. Ask; never fabricate the answer.
- **Empirical:** research, measurement, or observation can answer. Require evidence.
- **Low-cost reversible:** an agent may decide when scope and reversal are explicit.
- **Consequential:** record alternatives, rationale, and effective scope in DEC.

Use QST only when work is genuinely blocked or intentionally deferred. To defer,
record a DEC, blocking impact, and concrete reactivation condition. Do not hide
uncertainty in vague requirement language, treat silence as agreement, or call a
hypothesis a fact.
