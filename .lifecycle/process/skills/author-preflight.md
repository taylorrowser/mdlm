# Ephemeral author preflight

Before proposing authored Lifecycle Data, inspect the proposal in isolation using
the applicable independent-Review rubric and the exact Assignment packet.

1. Check contract validity, exact traceability, information allocation, scope,
   internal consistency, verifiability, and proportionality.
2. Treat a concern as blocking only when you can name the exact violated
   criterion, concrete evidence or counterexample, and the material consequence
   that prevents safe use. Correct every such concern before publication.
3. Correct clear `needs-triage` concerns when they are local and authorized.
   Do not expand scope to satisfy advisory preferences.
4. For a definition set, perform a deletion/merge pass and confirm every split is
   justified by distinct behavior, responsibility, boundary, risk, trust context,
   or verification need.

This preflight is bounded working memory, not Lifecycle Data. Publish no REV,
Finding, score, transcript, or telemetry from it. Do not place preflight content
in the response or Review Context, and do not supply it to the independent
reviewer. The later independent Review remains fresh-eyed and authoritative.
