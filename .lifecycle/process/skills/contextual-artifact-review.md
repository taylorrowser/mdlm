# Contextual artifact review

Judge one exact primary subject independently while using its frozen context.

1. Verify identity, schema, provenance, and allowed links.
2. Check fidelity to parents and consistency with siblings and decisions.
3. Apply the type rubric and challenge necessity and ambiguity.
4. Distinguish a defect in the primary subject from a collateral issue elsewhere.
5. Ignore any author preflight: it is neither supplied evidence nor Review input.
6. Mark a primary finding blocking only when it records the exact criterion,
   concrete evidence or counterexample, material consequence, target-local scope,
   and why safe use requires correction. For ambiguity, name two plausible
   materially different interpretations.
7. Use `needs-triage` or `advisory` for a usable subject; do not fail for optional
   clarity, style preference, or collateral concern.
8. Never repair the artifact during Review or allow prior acceptance to determine
   the judgment.

Create one REV per primary subject even when reviewing siblings in one session.
