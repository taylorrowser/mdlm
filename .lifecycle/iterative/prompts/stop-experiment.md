# Record the stakeholder's decision to stop exploration

Present the exact observation, useful learning and remaining failures to the
stakeholder. Record their explicit decision to end further exploration of this
observed branch, why the learning is sufficient, any chosen follow-up and the
source of the decision. Use actual stakeholder input or the demo's explicitly
recorded delegation. The fixed action is stop; ordinary correction remains
available until the stop decision is published.

Keep the linked observation and failed evidence unchanged. Stop does not claim a
criterion pass or product acceptance. Other unresolved trials and all selected
formal work retain their obligations. If the stakeholder chooses formal
correction, record that intent here and carry it into the existing requirements,
implementation, independent verification and acceptance route. This feedback
alone creates no formal correction record or requirement baseline.
