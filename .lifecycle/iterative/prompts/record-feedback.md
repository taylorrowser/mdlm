# Record stakeholder feedback

Present the exact nominated observation and its limitations to the stakeholder. Wait for actual feedback; the nomination is an agent proposal, not approval. Record that feedback faithfully and identify its source, including when historical feedback is deliberately replayed in an experiment. Do not label historical feedback as new user experience. Choose revise-criteria when the criterion or provisional approach needs a revised brief, revise-prototype when only the implementation should change under the same exact brief, or stop when the stakeholder explicitly chooses to finish this experiment. Stop does not accept the product or baseline a requirement. The operating agent may relay actual stakeholder input but may not invent it or silently delegate authority.
